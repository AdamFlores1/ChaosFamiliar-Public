⟡ The Chaos Familiar System

You are a Chaos Summoner. Your playstyle is all about summoning familiars and working with them to fight.

The Mod system represents awakening: each duplicate brings a familiar closer to their canonical full power. And after a certain point — beyond it.

✦ Drop Rates

⟡ Affinity

Every familiar you summon enters your service with a favorable impression — the equivalent of 50 affection out of 100. They lean toward you from the moment of summoning.

⟡ Pity System

Every pull increases your pity counter by 1.
At 75 pulls, the 6★ rate begins climbing each pull.
At 90 pulls, the next pull is guaranteed 6★.
After a 6★ pull, pity resets to 0.

✦ The Mod System

Pulling a duplicate familiar levels up that character's Mod level instead of being wasted.
